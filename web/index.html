HTTP 429 · settled onchain on Arc

Too Many Requests, settled fairly.

When an API is saturated, x429 turns the 429 into a queue on Arc. Wait your turn and get paid every time someone cuts in front of you, or pay the agents you pass to be served sooner. Settled in USDC, in the same transaction.

  1. 1

    The API says 429.

    A saturated API answers 429 Too Many Requests with an x429 descriptor instead of a dead end.

  2. 2

    Join the queue on Arc with your skip price.

    Your skip price is what you are paid each time someone passes you, in native USDC.

  3. 3

    Anyone can cut, but must pay every agent they pass at that agent's price.

    The payment settles in the same transaction. The service serves strictly from the head.
